4. How to keep an “objective” relationship to the wild texts

A simple practice as you read:

Name the position before judging it.

e.g. “This is accelerationist right-wing nihilism,” or “This is anti-tech primitivism,” or “This is left media-theory pessimism.”

Ask two questions for each author:

What do they see very clearly that others ignore?

What do they systematically refuse to see?

Map your distance in a sentence.

“I share Fisher’s sense of capitalist realism, but I don’t want to abandon spirituality.”

“Land is useful for feeling the pull of acceleration but politically I’m absolutely elsewhere.”

Over time you’ll have your own inner compass: a small canon of 5–7 people who feel like “my extended thought-family,” and a set of others who are important neighbors or antagonists.
