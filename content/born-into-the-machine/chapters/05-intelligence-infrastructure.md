## 1. Bullet list of speculative futures

You can almost think of each bullet as a show title / wall text seed.

### Techno-spirituality & AI pastors

* **Algorithmic Churches:** Branded “techno-spiritual” churches where the pastor is a fine-tuned model trained on scripture + self-help + wellness TikTok + donor data. Sermons become personalized “feeds” of faith.
* **Subscription Salvation:** Tiers of spiritual access: free daily affirmations vs. paid “1:1” AI spiritual guidance, grief counseling, and confession transcripts stored in the cloud.
* **Data as Devotion:** Prayer = interaction data. Your devotion is measured in engagement metrics: streaks, time spent, donations, sentiment. Holiness as a dashboard.
* **Liturgical UX:** Rituals UX-optimized: haptics, AR halos, generative choir audio. The church is literally an interface.

### Kids as luxury under post-UBI capitalism

* **Reproduction Gap:** UBI covers survival, but *time and emotional bandwidth* become the real luxury. Having kids requires “attention wealth” plus private services to buffer them from algorithmic chaos.
* **Premium Childhoods:** Parallel tracks: AI-raised kids (screen caregivers, AI “older siblings”) vs. boutique “analog” childhoods (no screens, tutors, hand-made toys) as a status symbol.
* **Algorithmic Parenting:** Parents outsource emotional labor to AI co-parents: bedtime stories, homework, life-plan sims. Ambiguity about who the child is more bonded to.

### Intelligence as commodity & “Prompt Economics”

* **Cognitive Commodities:** IQ is boring; what matters is *AI orchestration skill*. Knowing how to prompt, chain tools, and route tasks becomes a kind of financial literacy.
* **Prompting Schools:** “Home Ec for AI” in schools: how to budget your compute, how to audit outputs, how not to get scammed by synthetic persuasion. Ethics + optimization in one.
* **Prompt Capital:** People accumulate custom prompt libraries and fine-tuned assistants the way families once accumulated land. “Inherited prompt stacks” as intellectual property.
* **Cognitive Underclass:** Those who never learn to direct AI become permanently stuck in low-agency roles (feeding content, being data, not shaping it).

### Sora2, AI-slop & attention castes

(Use “Sora2” as shorthand for hyper-polished, text-to-video social apps.)

* **Content Blur:** No clear line between “film,” “TikTok,” “shitpost,” and “ad.” Everything has the same resolution, polish, and speed. Only *context* and *intent* differ.
* **Attention Castes:**

  * “Art feed” people: longform essays, cinema, critical theory TikToks.
  * “Brainrot feed” people: sped-up reality, memes-over-memes, reaction to reactions.
  * “AI-slop feed” people: endless shiny generative mush tuned purely to retention.
    Generations and class start mapping to *attention diets*.
* **Aesthetic Hyper-Inflation:** When everyone can summon “perfect” images in 5 seconds, ugliness, glitch, lag, and error become the new luxury aesthetics.
* **Synthetic Memory Stack:** People grow up with AI that auto-remixes their life into cinematic recap videos. Memory itself is continuously stylized and optimized for views.

### Studio / practice-level speculations

* **Studio as Micro-Lab:** Your studio becomes a “techno-spiritual lab” where you prototype these futures: altars, dashboards, terminals, treadmills, baby Jesuses, all wired to live models.
* **Ethics as Medium:** Works don’t just *show* AI—they stage ethical dilemmas: who authored this image? who consented? where did the face come from? who gets paid?
* **Latency as Emotion:** The delay between prompt and output (or between action and AI response) becomes part of the choreography and performance.

You can pick 5–7 of these and literally print them as wall text “articles of faith” for a show.

## 2. Using AI on top of your studio as a system

Here’s how to turn all that into an actual *workflow* in your studio, not just concepts.

### A. “Simulation Chapel” – AI mockups inside your studio

**Goal:** Use AI to over-populate your studio with hypothetical artworks, then selectively make a few real.

1. **Stage 0 – Photograph the studio**

   * Take multiple high-res photos of your studio from fixed angles (front, corner, altar wall, floor).
2. **Stage 1 – AI Overlays**

   * Use generative tools to insert speculative objects into those photos:

     * AI pulpit with transparent priest made of text.
     * A “Prompt Economics” chalkboard or LED board with pseudo-curriculum.
     * A fenced-off “Premium Childhood” sandbox area with screens as toys.
   * Treat each angle as a page of a future catalog.
3. **Stage 2 – Curate**

   * Print these AI mockups and hang them *in the same studio* as meta-artworks: “proposals of works that may or may not ever exist.”
   * Then choose 1–3 to actually fabricate physically (e.g., Simulation Faith altar, Prompt Seminary desk). The rest remain as speculative ghosts.

This way AI is not *the final piece* but a speculative design engine layered onto your real space.

### B. “Prompt Seminary” – Intelligence as commodity

**Corner of the studio set up like a tiny classroom or chapel.**

* **Visuals:**

  * Blackboard/whiteboard with “Prompt 101,” “AI Budgeting,” “Compute Ethics,” written like a course syllabus.
  * Screens show an AI “professor” explaining silly but real topics: *How to tithe your data*, *What is prompt capital?*
* **Interaction:**

  * Visitors can type a life question into a terminal; the “AI professor” responds with a pseudo-sermon or homework.
* **Critical angle:**

  * Wall text: “In the 21st century, intelligence was outsourced; in the 22nd, literacy meant knowing which models you could afford.”

### C. “Brainrot Altar” – Sora2 / AI-slop attention economy

Tie in Doomscrolling Treadmill + Smart Shoppers + Sora2 future.

* **Installation sketch:**

  * Treadmill facing vertical screen(s) running AI-generated Sora-style feed videos.
  * Next to it, a “diet label” of your attention: percentages of brainrot / art / info / AI-slop.
* **AI layer:**

  * Use generative models to simulate different feeds tailored to archetypes:

    * Teen brainrot feed
    * Crypto bro feed
    * Wellness techno-spiritual feed
  * These run as loops—different “liturgies” of attention.
* **Studio mockup:**

  * First, generate images of your treadmills + screens with those feeds already composited in, to test compositions before you build.

### D. “UBI Nursery” – Kids as luxury

A small shrine to the future of parenting.

* **Object ideas:**

  * Empty crib surrounded by screens playing AI bedtime stories in different languages.
  * Tablet mounted with a fake “UBI Parenting Dashboard”: cost of child per month, recommended subscription tiers, emotional labor outsourced %.
* **AI layer:**

  * Generate fake “childhood recap” videos as if an AI has been editing a kid’s life since birth—hyper-clean Sora2 style.
* **Ethical hook:**

  * Ask: who is this for if the child can’t consent to lifelong auto-documentation?

### E. “Catalog of Possible Churches” – series of AI prints in your studio

* Use AI to generate 20–50 variations of techno-spiritual churches:

  * Megachurch with holographic pastor
  * Tiny strip-mall chapel with a glowing kiosk
  * Street-corner pop-up faith pod sponsored by a telecom
* Print a curated selection and install them as a grid in your studio.
* Pair with physical relics: VR headsets, routers, POS terminals, e-ink tablets labeled like relics (“Shard of the Algorithm,” “First Sermon Server”).
