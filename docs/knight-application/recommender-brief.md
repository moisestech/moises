# Brief for recommendation letter — Knight Technology Product Strategist

Use this when asking someone to write to **recruiter@kf.org** (or to send you a PDF to forward, if they prefer).

## What the role needs (so the letter lands)

Knight is hiring for **technology product strategy** in journalism: evaluating **AI and emerging tech**, understanding **media consumption and distribution**, building **networks** of technologists and practitioners, and supporting **documentation, learning, and grant operations**. The letter should sound closer to **product, systems, and public information** than to “artist bio” alone.

## Ask them to include 3–4 concrete anecdotes

Pick prompts that match what they actually observed:

1. **Technical depth** — e.g. shipping or reviewing software, AI workflows, streaming/automation, debugging, architecture, or translating a vague idea into an implementable plan.
2. **Product judgment** — e.g. when you pushed back on a demo-heavy idea, scoped an MVP, improved UX, or clarified users and operators.
3. **Stakeholders / institutions** — e.g. workshops, labs, museums, startups, or community programs: reliability, communication, documentation, or running public-facing systems.
4. **Public representation** — e.g. talks, teaching, panels, or translating complex technology for mixed audiences (especially if they saw you with non-engineering publics).

## Tone

Direct, specific, and evidence-based. One paragraph on **why they believe** you can evaluate and support **technology investments for news and civic information**, even if your title has been artist or engineer rather than “journalist.”

## Timing (default)

- Best: recommender sends within **a few days** of your application email, so the inbox thread stays coherent.
- In your application email you may add one line: *“A recommendation from [Name] will follow under separate cover.”*

## What not to do

- Avoid vague superlatives without examples.
- Avoid implying formal endorsement by Knight or by institutions unless true.
- If startup work is under NDA, the recommender should describe **responsibilities and impact** without confidential product details.
