# Moonlighter chapter stills — prompts for images we cannot reuse

Do not generate these in the audit pass. Each prompt is for a teaching still the existing Cloudinary set cannot honestly cover.

When a still is made, add it to `src/content/workshops/moonlighter-ai-3d-printing/landing-media.ts` and point the matching slot in `chapter-media.ts` at that asset. Until then the chapter shows a labeled gap.

## Shared suffix

Append to every prompt:

> Moonlighter FabLab maker-lab documentary style, charcoal equipment and surfaces, restrained coral accent #FF6B5A, warm daylight, generous negative space for UI overlays, precise but human, no visible brand logos, no fake interface text, no watermark, no science-fiction holograms

## Do not prompt these

Photograph them when Moonlighter access exists. Do not generate stand-ins that claim to be the real machine, room, or failure.

- Exact P1S and H2D printers with unit labels
- The real room, computers, and workstation layout
- Real PLA spools and printed swatches
- Safety equipment and support-removal procedure
- Live instructor demonstration and consented participants
- Real print failures and reprints
- Pickup shelf and handoff
