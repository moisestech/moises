# Synthetic Practice Case — Northstar Studio

Use this case when a participant cannot or should not use real business information.

All people, clients, amounts, dates, and business details below are fictional.

## Business

Northstar Studio is a small owner-operated creative services and education business. It earns revenue from client projects, paid workshops, and occasional institutional contracts.

The founder handles:

- sales and institutional relationships;
- scoping and pricing;
- final creative decisions;
- curriculum decisions;
- high-risk approvals.

A small team/freelancers can handle:

- routine production;
- file preparation;
- scheduling coordination;
- status updates;
- documentation.

## Current 30-day outcome

Secure two new institutional projects while delivering the current client project on time and filling the next paid workshop.

## Working constraints

- The founder is available 9:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m.
- Deep work is strongest before 1:00 p.m.
- Client/institutional meetings often require 20–30 minutes of preparation.
- Friday afternoon is reserved for delivery/review.
- The founder should not send new scope, pricing, contracts, payments, or major schedule changes without reviewing them first.

## Where information lives

Calendar:
scheduled meetings, workshops, travel, hard commitments.

Email:
client conversations, proposals, follow-ups, institutional relationships.

CRM:
lead stage, opportunity value, last follow-up date, next step.

Accounting:
invoices, payment status, receivables.

Project board:
deliverables, owners, production state, blockers.

Messages:
some fast-moving client requests and collaborator updates.

- One existing client invoice is overdue.
- A high-value proposal is waiting on founder scope decisions.
- Workshop enrollment is below the minimum target.
- Friday client delivery still needs final approval.
- The CRM shows one qualified lead with no next step assigned.
- No protected focus block exists tomorrow morning.

## Example Attention Rules

### Commitments

Client delivery promises, paid workshops, scheduled institutional meetings, and deadlines with real external consequence outrank optional internal improvements.

### Revenue / Mission

Qualified opportunities, proposals, collections, paid workshop enrollment, and delivery that protects an active client relationship can earn attention.

### Risk

Anything that could cause a missed client delivery, broken promise, lost qualified opportunity, or financial collection problem should be surfaced.

### Capacity

Protect at least one uninterrupted focus block on a normal workday. Do not recommend more than three top outcomes. Optional research and website polish should not displace externally consequential work.

## Example Daily Operating Brief

### Today in one sentence

Protect Friday’s client delivery and move the institutional proposal while preserving one real focus block.

### Non-negotiable commitments

10:00 institutional scope call — requires clear scope questions and decision notes.

1:00 production check-in — final proof needs founder approval before Friday.

3:00 workshop planning — useful, but should not consume the open focus block.

### Top 3 outcomes

1. Approve or correct the production proof for Friday.
2. Turn the institutional call into a decision-ready proposal scope.
3. Follow up on the overdue invoice / qualified opportunity depending on which has the higher current consequence.

### Focus block

11:30–12:30 — proposal or production proof, whichever remains unresolved after the 10:00 call.

### What can wait

Software research.
Old-file cleanup.
Nonessential landing-page polish.

### One change

Protect tomorrow morning with a focus block before accepting another optional meeting.

## Example Fact / Interpretation / Recommendation

FACT:
Friday delivery still requires founder approval.

INTERPRETATION:
If approval slips, production may lose the remaining review window.

RECOMMENDATION:
Review the proof before using the open focus block for lower-consequence work.

## Example friction

Qualified leads require checking both email and the CRM to reconstruct the actual next step.

Classification:
retrieval + reconciliation.

Do not automate yet.

First improvement:
make the CRM next-step field mandatory after every meaningful sales interaction.

## Why this case exists

The synthetic case should let a participant complete the exact same exercises without exposing real business information.

It is intentionally simple enough for a nontechnical cohort and rich enough to support later Calendar, CRM, accounting, and workflow exercises.
