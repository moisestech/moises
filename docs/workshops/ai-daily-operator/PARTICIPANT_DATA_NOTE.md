# Participant Data & AI Use Note — Level I

Build Your AI Daily Operator is a hands-on workshop. You may use your own business information only when it is appropriate and safe to do so.

## Before sharing information with an AI tool

- Confirm you are using the intended account or workspace.
- Follow your employer, client, institution, or organization’s policies.
- Share only the information necessary for the exercise.
- Do not paste passwords, security credentials, payment card details, banking credentials, private keys, or authentication secrets.
- Do not paste confidential customer, employee, patient, student, legal, financial, or contractual records unless you are explicitly permitted to use that information in the selected AI environment.
- Prefer public, synthetic, aggregated, redacted, or appropriately de-identified information when possible.
- If you are unsure, use the provided synthetic practice case.

## Before acting on AI output

Treat polished output as a draft to review.

For important recommendations:

1. Trace the relevant facts to the information you provided or a trusted source.
2. Separate fact from interpretation and recommendation.
3. Recalculate important numbers.
4. Verify names, dates, prices, deadlines, policies, and promises.
5. Keep a person responsible for the final decision.

## Level I action boundary

Level I is designed for judgment and planning, not autonomous action.

The default policy is:

**RECOMMEND → EXPLAIN → ASK → ACT**

The workshop does not require participants to let AI:

- send external messages;
- reschedule meetings;
- make purchases;
- make payments;
- modify accounting;
- accept contracts;
- commit to client deadlines;
- make hiring or personnel decisions;
- reveal confidential information.

## Missing information

If the AI does not have an important piece of information, that information is **unknown**.

It should not be treated as false and should not be invented.

## Synthetic fallback

A fictional Northstar Studio case is available so every participant can complete the workshop without using real business data.

## Tool availability

ChatGPT and Claude features, connectors, plans, permissions, and interfaces can change. The workshop is designed so Level I works in ordinary chat without requiring integrations.
