# Host One-Pager — Build Your AI Daily Operator

**A 90-minute hands-on Founder Attention OS workshop with Moises Sanabria**

## The question

Most small-business owners and founders do not need another list of AI tricks.

They need a better answer to:

> **What deserves my attention today, and why?**

Build Your AI Daily Operator helps participants turn the business context they already have — goals, commitments, opportunities, risks, and capacity — into a concise operating brief they can use in ChatGPT or Claude.

No coding and no integrations are required for Level I.

## What participants build

During the session, each participant creates:

1. **Founder Profile** — the context AI needs to understand their work.
2. **Attention Rules** — how Commitments, Revenue / Mission, Risk, and Capacity should shape priorities.
3. **Daily Operator** — portable instructions they can use in ChatGPT or Claude.
4. **Daily Operating Brief** — a two-minute view of what needs them, why, and what can wait.
5. **First Friction Note** — one place where the business still depends on manual retrieval, memory, transfer, approval, or repeated judgment.

## What they learn

Participants practice how to:

- distinguish a business outcome from a task;
- give AI the right context without over-sharing;
- decide what should remain human;
- challenge an AI recommendation instead of accepting it;
- separate fact, interpretation, and recommendation;
- identify missing information rather than letting AI guess;
- preserve human approval before consequential action;
- recognize where future process improvement could create leverage.

## The 90-minute arc

**0–10** — Why productivity is not enough / choose one outcome  
**10–25** — Build the Founder Profile  
**25–35** — Write Attention Rules  
**35–45** — Build the Daily Operator  
**45–58** — Run a real day through it  
**58–67** — Challenge the ranking  
**67–74** — Fact / Interpretation / Recommendation  
**74–82** — Find one friction  
**82–88** — Choose one improvement  
**88–90** — Start the seven-day experiment

## Platform

Participants may use **ChatGPT or Claude**.

The methodology is tool-independent. Level I deliberately avoids requiring Calendar, email, accounting, CRM, or automation connections.

Those sources enter only in the deeper curriculum after the decision model works manually.

## Why this is different

This is not:

- a prompt-engineering class;
- an “automate your whole business” pitch;
- a generic AI Chief of Staff template;
- a tour of every feature.

The workshop starts with finite human attention.

The course asks:

**What has been promised?**  
**What economically or missionally matters?**  
**What becomes worse if ignored?**  
**What can this person actually carry?**

Then it makes the tradeoff visible.

## Human responsibility

The operating policy is:

**RECOMMEND → EXPLAIN → ASK → ACT**

Participants are taught not to let AI silently invent missing context or take consequential action without appropriate human approval.

## After Level I

Participants can run a seven-day experiment with their Daily Operating Brief.

For organizations that want to continue, the full Founder Attention OS curriculum progresses into:

- **Level II — Connected Operator:** sources of truth and permissions.
- **Level III — Business Pulse:** money / mission signals that change decisions.
- **Level IV — Friction Intelligence:** diagnose what repeatedly steals attention.
- **Level V — Operating System:** design one governed workflow with human approval.
