# Monique — Founder Attention OS Outreach Strategy

## Use only when the project is ready

Do not send merely because the relationship is warm.

Use this message only when:
- the Core vs Studio structure is clear;
- the public page is concise enough to share;
- facilitator scorecard exists;
- delegation-readiness gate exists;
- internal economics are role-based;
- at least one rehearsal or pilot has exposed the real failure points;
- the relationship has had at least one additional natural interaction beyond the original Scale Up session / DCC invite.

## Strategic objective

The first conversation is for **critique and fit discovery**, not recruitment.

We want to learn:
- does Monique see Founder Attention OS as complementary or competitive?
- where does she think the overlap is strongest?
- what part of the participant experience would she improve?
- would a joint pilot create more value than two adjacent workshops?
- does her involvement materially reduce founder-hours while preserving quality?

Do not lead with:
- 50/50;
- facilitator licensing;
- ownership;
- “I want you to teach my course”;
- a giant curriculum repository.

## Recommended first message

Hey Monique — I’ve been meaning to circle back on something I’ve been building.

Your Scale Up session was useful for me because the way you got people actively thinking about delegation and the value of their time overlaps with a problem I’ve been working on from a different angle.

I’ve been building **Founder Attention OS / Build Your AI Daily Operator** around a question that sits slightly upstream of automation: **what actually deserves the founder’s attention, why, what can wait, and what friction should become a better system.**

I’ve now got the methodology, participant toolkit, artifacts, assessment, facilitator structure, and a 90-minute Core / deeper Studio format taking shape.

I don’t want to assume it’s complementary just because I see some interesting overlap, and I definitely don’t want it to feel like I’m stepping on something that’s core to your work.

When the timing is good, I’d really value your read on it as someone who teaches this audience well. If you look at it and think there’s a real gap we could fill better together, maybe there’s a small pilot worth testing. If not, I’d still genuinely value the critique.

No rush at all — I mostly wanted to share it once it was developed enough to be worth your time.

## Shorter WhatsApp version

Hey Monique — I’ve been building something adjacent to what you were teaching at Scale Up and wanted to wait until it was developed enough to be worth sharing.

It’s called **Founder Attention OS / Build Your AI Daily Operator**. The core question is a little upstream of automation: **what deserves the founder’s attention, why, what can wait, and what friction should become a better system.**

Your class made me think there may be some really interesting overlap, but I don’t want to assume it’s complementary or step on something that’s core to your work.

When the timing is good I’d love your honest read on it. If you think there’s a real gap we could fill better together, maybe we test one small pilot. If not, the critique alone would be super useful.

## If she responds positively

Do not immediately negotiate.

Send:
- public course page;
- Level I summary;
- optional one-page host offer.

Then ask for 30 minutes focused on:
1. what feels overlapping;
2. what feels differentiated;
3. what she would change for nontechnical founders;
4. whether the Core vs Studio split makes sense;
5. whether one bounded co-facilitated pilot would be worth testing.

## If she says it overlaps heavily

Respond positively.

Do not defend territory.

Use the information to narrow Founder Attention OS toward:
- founder attention;
- operating judgment;
- evidence quality;
- source / permission architecture;
- friction intelligence;
- governed system design.

Keep the relationship alive through DCC regardless.

## If she is excited about teaching

Do not offer long-term facilitator status immediately.

Pilot ladder:

```
Observe / review
→ co-facilitate one bounded pilot
→ review outcomes + founder-hours
→ lead with oversight
→ only later consider independent delivery
```

## Success metric

The relationship is successful if it creates one or more of:

- better curriculum;
- better beginner facilitation;
- reduced Moises delivery time;
- new institutional distribution;
- referrals to the right operator;
- stronger DCC network effects.

It does not need to become a formal partnership to be valuable.
