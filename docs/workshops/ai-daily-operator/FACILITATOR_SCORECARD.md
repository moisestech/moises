## Fidelity

Score each line pass, partial, or miss. Add one sentence of evidence.

- Method fidelity. The session stayed on attention, judgment, and one improvement.
- Four-lens fidelity. Commitments, Revenue or Mission, Risk, and Capacity were used.
- Fact / Interpretation / Recommendation. Participants could separate them.
- Unknown stays unknown. Missing information was not filled in.
- Approval boundaries. Recommend, explain, ask, then act stayed visible.
- Beginner handling. A nontechnical participant could finish without an integration.
- Pacing. Core fit 90 minutes, or the overrun is named.
- No premature automation. The first friction stayed evidence.
