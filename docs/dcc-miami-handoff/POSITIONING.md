# Positioning — DCC Miami vs moises.tech

## The split

**moises.tech** is the artist site: selected works, research, exhibitions, grants for the art practice, curators, residencies.

**dcc.miami** is the cultural-technology practice: workshops, artist support, public programs, institutional systems, Infra24.

These two offering pages currently live on moises.tech and read as a personal consulting practice. On DCC they should read as **what Digital Culture Center Miami offers institutions**.

Moises Sanabria is founder and lead. Credit him. Do not make the page a personal portfolio.

## Three DCC doors (keep them distinct)

| Route | Audience | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/institutions` | Museums, nonprofits, digital staff | Technical review, focused projects, fractional systems work |
| `/artist-infrastructure` | Incubators, schools, artist programs | Guest sessions, co-taught modules, institutional pilots |
| `/infra24` | Technical buyers, RFPs | Product: signs, kiosks, portals, update workflows |
| `/workshops` | Public / artists | Bookable catalog of sessions |

`/partners` stays the “host a workshop” door. `/who-we-work-with` stays the audience map. New pages should **link** those, not absorb them.

## Voice rules

- Subject: **DCC Miami** / **we**.
- Credit: **Moises Sanabria, founder** — ICA Digital Producer (2019–2020), Technical Director of Digital at Oolite Arts (2025–2026).
- Artworks (Smart Shoppers, Doomscrolling, exhibitions): link **out** to `https://moises.tech/...`.
- Case studies that still live on moises.tech (`/oolite-arts`, `/ica-miami`, `/bakehouse`): link **out** until DCC has its own versions.
- Workshops that already exist on DCC: keep internal (`/workshops`, `/workshop/...`).
- Do **not** list DCC Miami as a client of itself.
- Do **not** use personal Calendly (`calendly.com/moisestech`) or `m@moises.tech` on these pages.

After DCC ships these two pages, moises.tech `/institutions` and `/artist-infrastructure` should **redirect or point** to dcc.miami so the artist site stops pitching services.
